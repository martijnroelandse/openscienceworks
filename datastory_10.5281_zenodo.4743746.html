Full narrative

Published in 2020 on Zenodo by Sebastián García, Agustin Parmisano, and María José Erquiaga, IoT-23 provides labeled network traffic from real IoT devices, including malware-infected and benign captures. The dataset was designed as infrastructure for the field: a reusable resource that researchers can cite, compare against, and build on when testing intrusion detection and traffic-analysis methods. The record shows sustained scholarly uptake, with citations spread across articles, conference papers, reviews, book chapters, and preprints.
