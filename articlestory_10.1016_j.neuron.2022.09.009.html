Full narrative

A brain atlas of synapse protein lifetime across the mouse lifespan has established itself as a high-impact contribution to synapse biology and systems neuroscience. The study’s central advance is methodological and conceptual: it quantified endogenous PSD95 lifetime at single-synapse resolution across the mouse brain and lifespan, creating a Protein Lifetime Synaptome Atlas that helps explain how synaptic maintenance and remodeling vary across time and space.
