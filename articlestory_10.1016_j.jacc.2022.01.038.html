Full narrative

Published in the Journal of the American College of Cardiology in 2022, this article examines how experimentally induced sleep curtailment under free access to food affects energy intake, energy expenditure, and regional body composition. Its contribution is notable because the study was framed around a gap in the experimental evidence, particularly the absence of data on body fat distribution. Supported by NIH funding, the work sits at the intersection of sleep medicine, obesity, and cardiometabolic research.
