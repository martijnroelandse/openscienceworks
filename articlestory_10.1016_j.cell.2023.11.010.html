Full narrative

Qian, Celiker, Wang, Guner-Ataman, and Boyden introduce temporally multiplexed imaging as a way to read out multiple dynamic signaling events in the same living cell using standard microscopes and genetically encoded reporters. The paper’s core contribution is methodological: it reframes fluorescent proteins not just as color channels, but as time-dependent signals with distinct switching behavior that can be used to encode several biological readouts in one experiment.
