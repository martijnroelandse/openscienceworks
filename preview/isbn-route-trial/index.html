Can books with only an ISBN get a BookStory?

ISBN route trial on 30 OAPEN booksWorking page · not listed on the site2026-10-23

We took 30 open access books from OAPEN that have no DOI (26 publishers, English, 2012 onwards) and built a story for each from the ISBN alone. For comparison we ran the same checks on 30 books that do have a DOI. A dot below means that source found something for that book.

What we learned

  1. Finding the book itself was the first gap. The Dutch trade database knew the title of 1 of 30 books. Using OAPEN and DOAB's own open metadata, all 30 resolve.
  2. Download numbers need a DOI. OPERAS, the download service OAPEN uses, only knows books by DOI. Crossref can find a DOI for about 18 of the 30, and OPERAS has data for 6 of those.
  3. Sources that work from titles and ISBNs hold up: OpenAlex citations, Wikipedia citations, YouTube and news find as much for ISBN-only books as for DOI books.
