About this bookAbout the authorsReviews

The fourth century was a crucial period in the history of the Goths, whose relations with the Roman Empire shifted from frontier warfare to settlement within the imperial provinces. This volume brings together the principal sources for that history in translation, with commentary.

The texts assembled here — the Passion of St Saba, Ulfila's letter, the fragments of Eunapius and the relevant sections of Ammianus Marcellinus among them — remain the essential documentary basis for the study of Gothic society, religion and the crossing of the Danube.

Peter Heather is Professor of Medieval History at King's College London. John Matthews is John M. Schiff Professor Emeritus of Classics and History at Yale University.
