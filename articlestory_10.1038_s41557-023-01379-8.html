Full narrative

The article addresses a practical bottleneck in PROTAC development: pomalidomide, a widely used cereblon recruiter, can drive unwanted degradation of zinc-finger proteins and other off-targets. By focusing on this design problem, the paper speaks directly to a major concern in targeted protein degradation and offers guidance that is relevant to both mechanistic studies and therapeutic design.
